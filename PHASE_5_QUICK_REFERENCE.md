### 3. Weak Openings - Exploitation Guide
**Three severity levels with specific strategies**

#### 🔴 CRITICAL WEAKNESS (Win rate < 25%)
- **Recommendation**: "MUST PLAY - Your primary weapon"
- **Color**: Red background
- **Strategy**: Master this line, study every position, play it frequently
- **Example**: "Only 20% win rate across 10 games - JACKPOT!"

#### 🟡 MAJOR WEAKNESS (Win rate 25-35%)
- **Recommendation**: "PRIORITY OPENING - Focus preparation here"
- **Color**: Orange background
- **Strategy**: Build solid preparation, play regularly to exploit
- **Example**: "32% win rate - clear target for improvement"

#### 🟠 VULNERABLE (Win rate 35-50%)
- **Recommendation**: "ALTERNATE LINE - secondary option"
- **Color**: Yellow background
- **Strategy**: Use as backup, consider for specific matchups
- **Example**: "45% win rate - solid but not primary focus"

**Each weakness shows**:
- Performance stats (Total games, Win rate, Wins, Losses)
- Up to 3 key positions where they struggle
- Actual game examples showing the losses
- Specific tactical recommendations

**How to use**:
1. Sort by severity (start with 🔴 CRITICAL)
2. Study the key positions shown
3. Copy FEN positions to study with an engine
4. Follow the recommended strategy
5. Build your preparation around these openings

---

### 4. Strong Openings - Preparation Guide
**Three threat levels with counter-strategies**

#### 🔴 EXTREME DANGER (Win rate ≥ 70%)
- **Recommendation**: "AVOID IF POSSIBLE - Opponent is a master"
- **Color**: Red background
- **Strategy**: Must study deeply + prepare sidelines + have concrete plans
- **Risk Level**: Very High - Only play if thoroughly prepared

#### 🟠 HIGH THREAT (Win rate 60-69%)
- **Recommendation**: "CAREFUL PREPARATION REQUIRED"
- **Color**: Orange background  
- **Strategy**: Study their wins, understand plans, prepare responses
- **Risk Level**: High - Respect their preparation

#### 🟡 SOLID UNDERSTANDING (Win rate 50-59%)
- **Recommendation**: "WELL-PREPARED - Alternative lines recommended"
- **Color**: Yellow background
- **Strategy**: Analyze their technique, understand ideas, prepare responses
- **Risk Level**: Medium - Can be challenged with solid preparation

**Each strong opening shows**:
- Key positions they master
- Successful games to analyze
- Specific counter-strategy recommendations
- Where to focus defensive preparation

**How to use**:
1. Review the threat levels (start with 🔴 EXTREME DANGER)
2. Study the key positions they DOMINATE in
3. Analyze their winning games to understand plans
4. Follow preparation recommendations:
   - EXTREME DANGER: Find sidelines or avoid
   - HIGH THREAT: Prepare concrete responses
   - SOLID: Can play with solid preparation

## How to Use This Report for Preparation

### Step 1: Profile Review (5 minutes)
- Read the opponent profile section
- Understand their overall style
- Note key statistics

### Step 2: Weakness Identification (5 minutes)
- Scan the weak openings section
- Identify 🔴 CRITICAL and 🟡 MAJOR weaknesses
- These are your TARGETS

### Step 3: Strength Assessment (5 minutes)
- Review strong openings section
- Note 🔴 EXTREME and 🟠 HIGH threat openings
- These need careful handling

### Step 4: Position Study (20-30 minutes)
- For each target weakness: copy FEN positions
- Paste into chess engine for deep analysis
- Understand the tactical patterns in their losses

### Step 5: Opening Selection (5 minutes)
- **Primary opening**: Use their major weakness
- **Secondary opening**: Use their moderate weakness
- **Avoid**: Their extreme danger / high threat openings

### Step 6: Game Preparation (as needed)
- Study their winning games in your main openings
- Prepare specific defensive resources
- Develop concrete plans for middle game
